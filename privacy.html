Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who uses Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool run by a single person (its owner) for their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data Clipper accesses

  • YouTube (owner's account only): with the owner's permission, Clipper uploads videos to the owner's channel, sets their thumbnails, and reads that channel's own video list and statistics (views, watch time, subscribers gained).
  • Public stream data: public live streams, public chat messages and public clips from the streamers it follows, used only to find highlight moments.

How data is stored and shared

  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Clipper does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • Short pieces of stream transcripts may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to help pick highlights and write titles. No Google or YouTube account data is sent there.

YouTube API Services

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings.
