Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool that turns highlights from live game streams into short vertical videos and posts them to its owner's own YouTube channel. Every video credits and links the original streamer.

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect data from other people.

Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read the channel's own statistics.
